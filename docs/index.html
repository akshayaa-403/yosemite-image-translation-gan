What you are looking at

Two generators are trained on unpaired photographs: a pile of summer shots of Yosemite and a separate pile of winter shots, with no correspondence between them. Nothing in the data says what any given summer photo should look like in winter. What holds the translation together is the cycle constraint — summer → winter → summer has to return the image you started with — so the network learns a reversible change of season rather than a plausible but unrelated winter scene.

The model here is the generator only (the discriminators are training scaffolding), exported to ONNX and executed by ONNX Runtime Web through WebGPU where available and WebAssembly otherwise. Expect a slightly washed-out look: it runs at the resolution it was trained at and the result is scaled back up to your image's size.

MIT licensed. Trained on the summer2winter_yosemite dataset from the CycleGAN authors.
